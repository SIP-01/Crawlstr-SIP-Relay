# Security Audit — Crawlstr-SIP-Relay — 2026-10-20 (re-run 2026-10-21)

## Summary
Kotlin/Android Nostr relay (fork of greenart7c3/Citrine) extended into a mobile SIP-01 indexing relay. The SIP-01 additions (§12.4 ingestion validation AFTER signature verification, spec-§13-vector-tested normalization, NIP-50 operators, federated sync with WiFi-only/limited-network modes) remain careful work. **Delta vs `audit/2026-10`: the HIGH is CONFIRMED AT CODE LEVEL and still unfixed. Upstream has not changed since (v3.2.0, 2026-09-18, remains latest).** Findings: 0 CRITICAL / 1 HIGH / 1 MEDIUM / 2 LOW.

## Findings

| CSR-H1 | HIGH | app/src/main/java/com/greenart7c3/citrine/server/CustomWebSocketServer.kt (`validateAuthEvent`, lines ~249–283) | **NIP-42 AUTH bypass, verified in source**: the function checks kind 22242, challenge, relay URL, and ±10-min freshness, but NEVER verifies the Schnorr signature — `connection.users.add(event.pubKey)` then grants that identity's access.
